Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We've got answers.

1. What areas do you serve?

We serve Moore, Oklahoma, and surrounding communities.

2. What services do you offer?

We offer lawn care, pressure washing, window cleaning, leaf cleanup, flower bed weeding, car washing, dumpster washing, house-number refreshes, and pet waste cleanup.

3. Do you offer free estimates?

Yes! Contact us with details about the service you need, and we'll provide an estimate.

4. Do you offer one-time services?

Yes! We offer both one-time cleanups and recurring maintenance services.

5. Do I need to be home while the work is being completed?

Not necessarily. As long as we have access to the area being serviced and any necessary instructions, we can usually complete the work while you're away.

6. How can I request service?

7. What forms of payment do you accept?

Contact us to confirm available payment options before scheduling.

8. Do you provide before-and-after pictures?

We can provide pictures of completed work when requested.
